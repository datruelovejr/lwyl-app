# Demo Flow for Marti (Willingboro)

## Opening (2 min)

"Marti, what I'm about to show you is your team's behavioral DNA. This isn't theory—it's data from the 31 assessments your people already took. I'm going to show you exactly where friction lives on your team, why it's costing you, and how to fix it."

---

## Screen 1: Dashboard (2 min)

**Show:** Org overview, team count, assessed count

**Say:**
"Here's Willingboro at a glance. 31 people assessed. What you're looking at is the starting point for every conversation about team performance."

---

## Screen 2: Friction Map (5 min) — KEY SCREEN

**Show:** Team friction visualization, highest-cost pairs

**Say:**
"This is where it gets real. See these connections? The red ones are costing you energy, time, and results every single day. These aren't personality conflicts—they're predictable friction points based on how people are wired. Let me show you your top 3..."

Pick 2-3 high-friction pairs to highlight

---

## Screen 3: Bridge Wizard (5-7 min) — THE WOW

**Show:** Select two people with high friction, walk through the 9-step process

**Say:**
"Now here's what we do about it. I'm going to pick [Person A] and [Person B]—you probably already know there's tension there. Watch what happens..."

**Walk through:**
1. Friction type (Preference, Passion, or Process)
2. Impact statement - owning your side
3. Bridge insights - specific commitments
4. Repair protocol - what to do when it breaks

"This isn't a one-time fix. It's a protocol. When things break—and they will—both people know exactly how to repair it."

---

## Screen 4: Individual Profile (2 min)

**Show:** Marti's own profile or a key leader

**Say:**
"And it starts with self-awareness. This is what your people see about themselves. Their communication style, what drains them, what energizes them. When everyone on your team understands this about each other, the friction drops."

---

## Close (2 min)

"Marti, you've got 31 people. That's 465 possible relationship pairs. Some of those are costing you right now—in turnover, in conflict, in meetings that go nowhere. This tool shows you exactly which ones to fix first and gives you the protocol to do it. That's the value."
