# Values Construct Verification

Purpose: Confirm the construct definition of each of the seven Values Index dimensions against both the licensed instrument and the academic source, before sorting any value into a friction bucket. This document is the verified reference. Build the competition sort off this, not off memory or surface word meaning.

Verified: June 2026. Sources listed at the end.

---

## Why this verification was needed

Two construct errors were caught. The first was reading the Economic value by the everyday meaning of the word, money and budgets, instead of its real construct, usefulness and practical return. The second was a swap inside the OrgHarmony Methodology Foundation file, where Individualistic and Political had each other's definitions. Both are corrected and documented below.

## The Economic correction, stated plainly

The construct is the drive for usefulness and practical return. It is the "is it worth it" instinct applied to time, energy, and effort, not money. Spranger tied it to a utilitarian ethic, where an action is good because it produces practical benefit. Money is the most visible expression of the drive, and the instrument label itself leads with the word, which is the trap. The core is efficiency and payoff, not finance.

Consequence for the sort: two high-Economic people are not mainly fighting over a pool of cash. They both push the practical, efficient, results-first path. That sits closer to a whose-approach-wins clash than to a scarce-resource grab. Any earlier "shared budget" rule for Economic is void. It was built on the wrong premise.

## The Individualistic and Political swap

The OrgHarmony Methodology Foundation file had these two crossed. It listed Individualistic as "personal power, influence, control" and Political as "position, advancement, competitive advantage." The instrument says the opposite.

Correct definitions:
- Individualistic is uniqueness and independence, the drive to stand apart and have freedom of personal expression.
- Political is the power, influence, and control drive, the leadership and competitiveness value.

Status: Fixed in OrgHarmony_Methodology_Foundation.md, rows for Individualistic and Political. The dimension-naming formula elsewhere in that file was left untouched because it only references the names, not the definitions.

Why it matters: if the sort is built off the crossed file, the wrong value gets routed to the Split Ownership bridge. The competition logic depends on these two being defined correctly.

---

## Honest uncertainty

Individualistic is Innermetrix's own split from the classic six values, so it carries thinner independent academic grounding than Political or Economic. Treat its competition behavior as signal until observed in real pairs.

Regulatory maps only loosely onto Spranger's categories. Lean on the instrument's own definition for it rather than the academic root.

Political and Altruistic are the safest to build on, because instrument and academic source agree cleanly. Economic is safe now that it is corrected.
