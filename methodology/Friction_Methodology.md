# Friction Methodology

**Status:** Single source of truth. Finalized for application to the app.
**Date:** June 6, 2026.
**Authority:** Built across Stages 00 to 06, validated at every step by orgharmony-psychometric-validator v6.0, reviewed by a separate blindspot-reviewer agent. Governed by Friction_Definition.md, the Scarcity Test, and the Coverage-Gap Demand Test.
**What this is.** The complete, exhaustive map of how OrgHarmony identifies and measures friction across DISC, Values, and Attributes, at four scopes, plus hiring and termination. When this is finalized, friction is never re-mapped. The app builds from here.

---

## 1. What friction is

Friction is the cost that appears when two trait profiles meet a shared demand. It takes two people, never one. It needs a shared demand, a decision, a task, a role. And it is a cost, the energy, time, or quality lost.

Friction is trait-derived and stable. It does not move when people try harder. What moves is the realized cost, measured through behavioral adherence and Goal Attainment Scaling, not by re-scoring the friction.

Friction comes from two families. A family is a group of friction types that share a root cause, which means they share a fix.

**Approach-clash** runs on EBIB. Each person's lens, built from environment, beliefs, identity, and proven methods, makes their own way feel right and the other's feel wrong. The fix is lens reconciliation.

**Structural** does not run on EBIB. The cost comes from a scarce resource or an unmet demand, not from anyone's lens. No one is wrong. The fix is structural.

**The rule that prevents the old contradiction.** EBIB applies to approach-clash only. It never applies to the structural family. Telling two people who both want control to understand each other does nothing, they already share the view, they need the role carved up. Prescribing lens-understanding for a structural problem is the error that broke earlier builds.

## 2. The four sources, the master map

| Source | Family | What it is | Detection rule, from scores | Confirmation input | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Difference | Approach-clash | Different trait levels, approaches read as wrong | Distance, gap past the tier | None, scores confirm it | Translation |
| Whose-standard-wins | Approach-clash | Same high trait, different version of right | Co-elevation, both in the high band | Do their standards differ | Set the Standard |
| Competition | Structural | Both high, both reach for one scarce thing | Co-elevation on a finite-resource trait | Is the resource scarce and shared | Split Ownership |
| Coverage-gap | Structural | Both low on a needed capacity, no one covers it | Co-suppression, External only | Does the role demand it, with no complement | Create Systems, Hire Talent, or Delegate |

**The fix names, in plain words.**

**Translation.** Help each person see the other's way. For Difference.

**Set the Standard.** Decide whose rule the team follows when both care about the same thing but run it differently. Adopt one rule, or give each a lane. For Whose-standard-wins.

**Split Ownership.** Divide the one scarce thing so each owns a piece. For Competition.

**Create Systems, Hire Talent, or Delegate.** Build a complement around a hole no one covers. For Coverage-gap.

A note for anyone reading the rigor skill, it still uses the older I/O terms allocation and adjudication inside its engine. Those mean exactly Split Ownership and Set the Standard, in that order.

## 3. The one frame that makes the map complete

Distance only sees friction when two people sit far apart. The moment they sit close, distance reads near zero and goes quiet. But close is not always calm. Both high, they fight over one thing or over whose way is right. Both low, the work goes uncovered.

So the four sources cover the whole field. Difference covers far apart. The same-pole sources cover both agreement ends, high and low. There is no fifth region for a fifth source to hide in. This is the exhaustiveness proof, and Section 10 returns to it.

## 4. Per-instrument application

Difference runs on all dimensions of all three instruments. The same-pole sorts below come from the scarcity test, can both people be fully satisfied on this target at once. Yes is a quality, whose-standard. No is a unit, competition.

**DISC.** D competes over the one call. I competes weakly over the spotlight, a soft signal. S rarely fires same-pole, two steady people align. C is whose-standard over the correct process. A both-low DISC team can leave a behavioral demand uncovered, a derived coverage-gap, weaker than the attribute form because a low DISC score is a style, not a deficit.

**Values.** Political is dual-source, the one leadership seat is competition, the right way to lead is whose-standard, route each part. Economic is whose-standard by default, the efficient path is a quality, and it gains a competition edge only when one shared pool exists. Economic is the utility drive, not money. Individualistic competes weakly over being the distinct one, a signal. Regulatory and Aesthetic are whose-standard, order and form are qualities. Altruistic and Theoretical bond when both are high, bond means no source fires, and they show a coverage-gap when both are low. Individualistic and Political stay separate, uniqueness versus power.

**Attributes.** No competition, a capacity is not a single unit, two people can both be high on empathy at once. No separate whose-standard, the bias mechanism already carries directional conflict. Coverage-gap is the primary home, External only, the three dimensions Empathy, Practical Thinking, and Systems Judgment. Difference on attributes is score gap plus bias direction, take the more severe, never score alone.

**Internal Impact Friction is real friction.** Difference runs on the three Internal attributes too, Self-Esteem, Role Awareness, Self-Direction, as score gap plus bias, take the more severe. That is Internal Impact Friction, a genuine dyadic friction, and the live engine already computes it. Two people with opposite Self-Direction biases clash over autonomy. It is kept separate from the External Process read and routed to a self-concept intervention, not to the External Process Agreement, because Internal friction needs self-concept help and External friction needs process help. Merging the two hides which kind of help is needed.

**The External-only boundary on coverage-gap, absolute.** Coverage-gap never touches the three Internal attributes. You cannot hire, delegate, or systematize a person's self-concept. So a shared LOW on Internal is not a coverage-gap. At team scope a shared low routes to KRI, a retention indicator, and at the individual level to personal development. This is the both-low case. It is different from Internal Impact Friction, which is the difference-and-bias clash case, and which is friction. KRI is not friction. Coverage-gap is not Internal. Internal Impact Friction is friction.

## 5. The four scopes, plus hiring and termination

Build up one level at a time. Dyadic feeds team. Team feeds organization. Never read individuals or pairs straight into the org.

**Dyadic.** Two people, one shared demand. The base unit. All four sources run here first.

**Team.** The two approach-clash sources roll up pairwise, aggregate every pair by dimension and rank the priority areas. The two structural sources need team-native operators, because they are group properties, not sums of pairs. Competition counts the high-band contenders on one shared unit, severity rising with the count. Coverage-gap fires when fewer than 40 percent of the team clear the bar on a demanded External capacity, read by cluster and rank.

**Organization.** Aggregate the team layer, never individuals. The org view adds what a team cannot see, the culture blind spot the org over and under-selects for, leader-team cost, cross-team patterns versus single-team composition, and competition over org-scarce units like one promotion. It needs three or more teams and fifteen or more people to be honest.

**Hiring.** Project a candidate into a named pairing, a team, and the org, and run all four sources forward. Coverage-gap is the selection signal, the candidate who clears the bar on a capacity the team lacks. Added competition risk is the mirror, another high on a contested scarce unit. Show both, never net them into one number.

**Termination.** Run the engine backward. Re-run the team without the person and name the External coverage that drops below the bar, the capability that walks out. Show the friction relieved too, for the whole ledger. External only here as well.

## 6. Confirmed versus signal, the status of every claim

This is the honesty layer. Only one source is confirmed from scores. Every other source is a signal until its confirmation input fires. And every high-low call needs the instrument band, which is not yet stored, so the band-dependent reads carry a second block.

| Source and scope | Status | Why, and what it waits on |
|---|---|---|
| Difference, all scopes | Confirmed | Distance on stored scores. The one source that needs no confirmation input. Bands are not required, it reads gaps, not high-low |
| Internal Impact Friction | Confirmed | The Difference source on the three Internal attributes, score gap plus bias. The 0 to 10 scale with bias is stored, so it runs now. Routed to a self-concept intervention, kept separate from the External Process read |
| Whose-standard, all scopes | Signal | Needs the standards-differ check, and needs bands to detect the both-high precondition. Bands not stored |
| Competition, all scopes | Signal | Needs the scarcity and shared-unit confirmation, and needs bands for the both-high gate. Bands not stored |
| Coverage-gap, all scopes | Signal | Needs the role-demand and complement confirmation. Attributes use the 0 to 10 scale with bias, which is stored, so the score read works, the demand input is what is missing |
| Hiring and termination | Decision support, not a verdict | Decision validity is UNESTABLISHED until production outcomes exist. Never the sole criterion for a hire or a firing |

**The banding standard.** Every high and low comes from the instrument's own validated band, per dimension, never an invented cutoff. A 48 can read High on one value and a 49 Average on another. The current engine still uses a hard-coded 60 cutoff and must move to the band read once bands are captured.

## 7. Operators and thresholds, locked

**Difference.** Sum of absolute Natural gaps per instrument. Tiers at 40, 80, 140 for the summed gap. The Values per-dimension read also uses a 20 and 40 cut. Natural scores, never Adaptive. The 40 line is 3.3 to 5 times the DISC SEM. The attribute difference uses score gap plus bias, take the more severe, on both the External and the Internal dimensions, the Internal read being Internal Impact Friction. A gap is a distance, so these tiers are not high-low calls and stand on their own. Open question for calibration, whether the gap tier should become SD-relative rather than fixed points.

**Competition.** A gated minimum. Gate, both contenders in the instrument high band, plus a shared scarce unit. Intensity, the band of the weaker contender. Then times the per-dimension scarcity weight, full for D and the Political seat, reduced signal for I and Individualistic, off for Economic unless a shared pool, zero for all quality targets. Minimum over product is a reasoned operational choice, held for calibration.

**Coverage-gap.** Co-suppression, External only. Dyadic, the three External attributes with bias. Team, fewer than 40 percent clear the bar, read the 78 by cluster and rank, filtered to External. The kind of gap shapes the fix, capability, drive, or behavior. One provenance guardrail, the coverage-gap 40 percent, the share clearing the bar, is a different test from the KRI 40 percent, the share low with a minus bias. They share the number and measure different things. Do not merge them.

**No fit discount, ever.** A style gap is friction, not fit. A high-low pair keeps full difference friction and only skips the one power fight two highs would have had.

## 8. Decision validity and fairness, where the method acts on people

Routing, leader flagging, hiring, and termination all act on people. Consistency is not decision validity. A repeatable score can predict nothing.

**The verdict, today.** Decision validity is UNESTABLISHED until production outcomes exist. So these reads are decision support, never a verdict. No people-decision rests on a friction score or a coverage-fill alone before validation.

**Adverse impact.** The four-fifths rule. For positive selection, hiring, flag any protected group selected below 80 percent of the top group's rate. For an adverse action, termination, flag any group terminated at a disproportionately high rate. The directions differ and the difference matters. This check needs no production outcomes, but it does need protected-group data, which the current schema does not store. So it is blocked until that data is captured, and capturing it is a high priority given the K-12 exposure.

## 9. The calibration plan, tied to the first twenty bridges

Most of what stays at signal becomes confirmed only with production data. The first twenty production bridges are the calibration set. For each, log four things, the friction read, the confirmation-input answer, the intervention run, and the realized outcome by behavioral adherence and Goal Attainment Scaling.

That data calibrates, in order of what unlocks first.

**Available now, from platform data.** Selection ratio, the share of candidates advanced or employees flagged. It needs only the cut applied to the population, no demographic data and no outcomes.

**Available as soon as the data layer is built, no outcomes needed.** Adverse impact, once protected-group data is captured. The band read, once bands and norms are captured, which also retires the legacy 60 cutoff.

**Available from the first cohort.** Base rates for hiring and for termination. How often a hire succeeds, how often a predicted capacity loss actually materializes.

**Available as the cohort matures, the Stage 07 calibration window.** Criterion validity for hiring and termination, classification accuracy, incremental validity against a cheaper screen, and utility. Plus the reasoned thresholds, the competition scarcity weights, minimum versus product, the fewer-than-40-percent line, the org minimum N, the difference-gap SD-relative question, and the weak signals on I, Individualistic, and the Economic shared-pool edge.

Until the calibration set returns, the signal-level sources stay labeled as signals, and the methodology says so plainly rather than overclaiming.

## 10. Completeness check, against the exhaustiveness requirement

The workspace required an exhaustive map, nothing friction-related left to map. Here is the proof.

**The score space is fully covered.** Every pair is far apart or close. Far apart is Difference. Close splits three ways. Both high, both low, or both in the middle. Both-high splits by the scarcity test into a unit, Competition, or a quality, Whose-standard. Both-low is Coverage-gap, on External, or it is a KRI or self-concept matter, on Internal. Both in the middle is no friction, no source fires. So every region has a home, and the four sources leave no friction uncovered.

**Difference covers all dimensions, and Internal Impact Friction is part of it.** Difference runs on DISC, Values, External attributes, and Internal attributes. On the Internal attributes it is named Internal Impact Friction and routed to a self-concept intervention. It is friction, not a fenced-off construct, and it is confirmed from stored data.

**Every instrument is covered.** DISC, Values, and Attributes each have their sorts in Section 4, with the coverage-gap External-only boundary holding throughout.

**Every scope is covered.** Dyadic, team, organization, and the two people-decision applications, hiring and termination, forward and inverse.

**The genuinely non-friction constructs are named and fenced off, so they are not mistaken for friction.** Bond is no source firing. KRI is a retention indicator. The Preference Tax is an individual-level cost, not a dyadic friction. Internal Impact Friction is NOT in this list, it is friction.

**One honest scope limit.** The methodology measures friction per instrument and per dimension and never composites, by the foundation's rule. So a friction that emerges only from two instruments interacting, DISC and Values together, is out of scope by design. Within each instrument and dimension, the map is complete.

**Conclusion.** Friction is exhaustively mapped within the per-instrument, per-dimension frame. Nothing friction-related is left to map. The methodology is complete and ready to apply.

## 11. Implementation requirements, the build list

These are what the app must add or change to run the full methodology. None of them changes the methodology, they make it runnable.

1. Capture the instrument band per dimension, and obtain the per-dimension norm tables to backfill stored raw scores. Retire the hard-coded 60 cutoff.
2. Add a role-capability demand reference, so coverage-gap and selection can judge whether a capacity is required.
3. Add a shared-unit and shared-pool context input, so competition fires only on a real shared scarce thing.
4. Capture protected-group data, so adverse impact can run on hiring and termination.
5. Log each projection and intervention, and later attach the outcome, so decision validity can be computed for hiring and termination.
6. Decide whether to store the full 78 attributes or run team coverage-gap on the six core dimensions, the six are the safe default until the 78 is captured.
