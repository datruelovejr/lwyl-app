# The Demand Test (Both-Low Coverage-Gap Rule)

**Status:** Reference rule for coverage-gap. Decides when a both-low case is a real gap.
**Date:** June 5, 2026
**Sits under:** Friction_Definition.md. This defines the demand reference the foundation named as the confirmation input for coverage-gap.

---

## What this resolves

When two people both score low on the same trait, or a team falls below the threshold on a capacity, the score shows shared absence, not whether that absence matters. This rule decides when a both-low case is a real coverage-gap and when it is harmless, and it names where the answer comes from so it is never left to interpretation.

---

## How this differs from the high rule

Both-high splits into two friction types, competition or whose-standard, because a shared want can be a unit or a quality. Both-low does not split. It is one type, coverage-gap. Nobody is reaching, so there is no clash over a prize and no clash over whose way is right. The only question is whether the missing thing actually matters. So this is a fire test, not a sort. Does the gap count, yes or no.

---

## Definition of "a real gap"

A shared low is a real gap when the demand requires a capacity that no one present supplies.

Two conditions, both must be true.

Required. The shared work activates this capacity. The demand reference answers this.

Uncovered. No person, system, or delegate already supplies it. The complement check answers this.

If either is false, the shared low is harmless. A capacity no one needs is not a gap, and a capacity already covered by a system or a delegate is not a gap. This is the manufactured-weakness construct applied to a pair or a team. A blind spot becomes a real weakness only when the role activates it and no complement exists.

---

## The non-interpretive test

A yes or no: does the shared work require this capacity, and is it currently uncovered?

No, not required, or already covered. Harmless, not a gap.

Yes, required and uncovered. Real coverage-gap.

The test is whether the demand activates the capacity, not how low the scores are. Two people can both score near zero on something the work never asks for, and nothing breaks.

---

## How we determine it

Need is not in the score. The score shows shared absence, the capacity is not present in these people. It cannot tell you whether the role needs that capacity or whether something else already covers it. Those come from two places, neither of them the score.

The demand reference gives requirement. It maps the role or the shared work to the capacities it actually calls for. This is the confirmation input for coverage-gap, and it is the piece still to be built. Until it exists, required is a judgment, not a lookup.

The complement check gives coverage. Before calling a required low a gap, look for a person outside the pair, a system, a process, or a delegate that already supplies it. A covered requirement is not a live gap.

---

## Detection level

Dyadic. Both people score low on the trait.

Team and organization. Use the share of the group that clears the bar. Below the 40 percent threshold, the capacity is effectively absent at the group level. Read the 78 Core Attribute List by cluster and rank, not by raw individual score. The six core dimensions give the faster, more reliable read.

---

## What goes missing when a trait is low

This is knowable now from the trait's target object. Whether it matters waits on the demand reference.

| Trait or capacity | What is missing when shared-low | Kind of gap | Solve flavor |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | No one drives decisions or takes the lead | Behavior | Assign the lead, create a decision rule |
| I | No one energizes the group or builds relationships | Behavior | Assign the connector, hire for it |
| S | No one holds steadiness or follow-through | Behavior | Create cadence and systems |
| C | No one guards detail, accuracy, or process | Behavior | Create checklists, hire detail |
| Empathy (External) | No one reads people | Capability | Hire or assign the people lens |
| Practical Thinking | No one drives pragmatic results | Capability | Create process, hire a doer |
| Systems Judgment | No one sees strategy or the big picture | Capability | Hire or assign the strategic lens |
| Altruist | No one is driven to serve or care | Drive | Assign accountability, hire for the drive |
| Theoretical | No one questions or tests the truth | Drive | Assign, hire |
| Regulatory | No one drives order or structure | Drive | Create systems |

The kind of gap shapes the solve. A capability gap means no one can do it, so build a system, hire, or delegate. A drive gap means no one is motivated to do it even if able, so assign accountability or hire for the motivation. A behavior gap means no one naturally does it, so name the role and build the cadence.

---

## The boundary that protects Internal versus External

Coverage-gap applies to External capacities, drives, and behaviors the group draws on. It does not apply to Internal attributes, Self-Esteem, Role Awareness, Self-Direction, because those describe how a person relates to themselves, not a capacity the team shares. You cannot delegate someone's self-esteem. A shared low on an Internal attribute is a personal-development matter, not a team gap. This keeps the Internal versus External rule intact.

---

## The case rule

A shared low fires as a real gap only when the demand activates the capacity and no complement covers it. Absent the demand, the low is dormant. With a complement in place, the low is handled. Both conditions must hold at once.

---

## The chain, stated once

Scores flag both-low, or the team below the 40 percent threshold. The demand reference decides whether the capacity is required. The complement check decides whether it is uncovered. Both-low is the signal. The demand reference and the complement check are what turn it into a verdict.

Solve follows. A confirmed gap routes to Create Systems, Hire Talent, or Delegate. In hiring, the candidate who supplies the missing capacity closes the gap, which is coverage-gap used as a selection signal.

---

## Honest uncertainty

The demand reference does not exist yet. It is the open piece that makes required a lookup instead of a judgment, and it is the same input the foundation flagged. Build it before this rule is fully operational.

The 40 percent threshold is borrowed from existing TeamInsights logic and needs validation against outcomes.

The granular 78 attributes are noisier than the six dimensions, so read them by cluster and rank, never by a single raw score.

The kind-of-gap split, capability versus drive versus behavior, is a reasoned distinction that sharpens the solve. Stress-test it against real teams before treating it as fixed.
