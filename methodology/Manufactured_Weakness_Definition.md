# Manufactured Weakness
### ADVanced Insights by Innermetrix | Attribute Index Framework

---

## Definition

A manufactured weakness is a blind spot that has been activated by role demands.

---

## Key Distinction: Blind Spot vs. Manufactured Weakness

The Innermetrix system draws a clear line between the two terms.

A **blind spot** is a non-talent that develops early in life. It is a low-scoring attribute on the Attribute Index. On its own, it is neutral. It does not cause harm simply by existing.

It becomes a **manufactured weakness** when two conditions are both true:

1. The role the person is in requires that attribute heavily.
2. No complement system exists to fill the gap.

The weakness is not inherent to the person. The role manufactured it by demanding what the person does not naturally lead with.

---

## Core Principle

You did not arrive with the weakness. The environment created the conditions for it to become one.

This reframes the entire conversation about low-scoring attributes. The question is never "what is wrong with this person?" The question is "what is the role demanding that this person does not naturally lead with, and what complement system is in place to cover it?"

---

## The Genius Level Leadership Prescription

For each attribute in the bottom tier of the full 78-attribute Core Attribute List, there are three possible responses:

- **Create Systems** — Build a structure that removes the reliance on that attribute.
- **Hire Talent** — Bring in someone for whom that attribute is a natural strength.
- **Delegate** — Assign the tasks that require that attribute to someone better equipped for them.

The goal is not to eliminate the blind spot through development. The goal is to build a complement system around it so the weakness never gets manufactured in the first place.

---

## Foundation Research

This definition is grounded in the Genius Study finding: the highest performers are not the most talented people. They are the people who know their deficiencies and build around them.

Awareness is the prerequisite. Without it, the blind spot stays invisible, the role continues to demand it, and the weakness compounds over time without the person understanding why.
