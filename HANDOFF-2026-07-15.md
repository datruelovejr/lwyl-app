## 1. How Daniel works, read first

- **He decides by reacting to a clean mockup, not a wall of text.** Build the visual, open it, let him react. Methodology fidelity is not design. Lead with the fast read, contain the long lists.
- **One question at a time.** Reflect back what you heard first, then ask one targeted question with a recommendation. Only for a true judgment gap. Never stack.
- **Hand him the raw material and the controls, not a finished conclusion.** This was the sharpest lesson this session. A cherry-picked verdict with no context gets rejected. Show the whole surface and let him drive. See Section 4.
- **Explain the why and the behind-the-scenes before you build.** He will stop you to understand the mechanics. Explain in fifth-grade language, with the why, before writing code.
- **Resolve versus ask.** If the research and constructs settle it, resolve it with the validator and show the reasoning. Ask only for his judgment, preference, missing data, or a real fork.
- **Follow the science, not the word.** If a tool contradicts the axiology or the instrument, stop and fix the tool first. The everyday-word trap bit the validator itself on Empathy.
- **Style, no exceptions.** No em dashes, no semicolons, no ellipses. Active voice. Short paragraphs, three sentences max. Contractions. Plain words in anything he reads. Real framework names on screen, never invented labels. Strength leads, never the deficit.
- **Process for methodology changes.** Validator, then a separate clean-window blindspot-reviewer, then fix, then stop for his approval.

Full guide: `core-attributes-workspace/rules.md`.

## 5. The core finding, the app runs two friction models at once

There are two friction vocabularies live in the app at the same time.

**The correct four-type model** lives in `src/app/utils/friction-report.js`, `teamFrictionReport(people)`. It names Difference, Whose-Standard, Competition, Coverage-Gap, plus Retention kept separate. It is used by exactly one screen, **The Method** (`components/MethodView.jsx`).

**The old, retired model** lives in `src/app/utils/friction.js`, `calculateFriction(a, b)`. It names Preference, Passion, Process, Internal Impact. It is used by **every other friction screen**: `FrictionMap.jsx`, `LeaderInsights.jsx`, `CompareWithOthers.jsx`, `app/page.jsx`, `app/dashboard/page.jsx`, `app/bridge/page.jsx`. The "preference tax" language Daniel already told us to kill still appears in `AuditDashboard.jsx`, `EnvironmentReport.jsx`, `IndividualComparison.jsx`, `LeaderInsights.jsx`, `RetentionRisk.jsx`.

So what Daniel disliked is not a wording slip. The majority of the app still runs the retired model.

### The four types, as the app runs them today

| Type | Engine state | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Difference** | Built, confirmed, correct | DISC and Values score gaps between two people |
| **Whose-Standard** | Built as a signal, but a rough proxy. Counts the share of the team high on DISC C or Regulatory, not the real both-high on the same quality test | DISC, Values |
| **Competition** | Built as a signal, but a rough proxy. Counts the share high on DISC D or Political, not the real both-high on a shared scarce unit test | DISC, Values |
| **Coverage-Gap** | Built as a signal. External attributes only. Correctly labeled, correctly walls off Internal | External Attributes only. No DISC, no Values |
| **Retention** | Built, kept separate, correct | Internal, Self-Esteem |

Engine files, all correct and reusable: `utils/{friction.js, coverage-gap.js, friction-report.js, samepole.js, bands.js, attr-tiers.js, strengths.js, team-attributes.js}`.

## 6. How coverage-gap connects to DISC, Values, and the four moves

- **The methodology defines coverage-gap across all three instruments.** `methodology/Coverage_Gap_Rule.md` maps it: DISC is a behavior gap, no one naturally does it, solve by naming the role and a cadence. Values is a drive gap, no one is motivated, solve by assigning accountability or hiring for the drive. External Attributes is a capability gap, no one can do it, solve by create, delegate, or hire.
- **"External only" means it excludes the three Internal attribute dimensions** (Self-Esteem, Role Awareness, Self-Direction), not "attributes only". DISC and Values are outward-facing and feed coverage-gap.
- **Read each instrument on its own, never composited into one number.**
- **The role demand is coverage-gap's confirmation input.** Both-low is the signal. The demand reference (required) plus the complement check (uncovered) turn it into a verdict. `Coverage_Gap_Rule.md` says this reference "is the piece still to be built." The Stage 03 role-demand tagging is that piece.
- **The four moves are the solve.** A confirmed coverage-gap routes to Create Systems, Delegate, or Hire. Own is the strength side.
- **Honest limit.** The DISC and Values side of coverage-gap is sketched, not nailed. The rule flags its trait mappings as "a reasoned read, not a validated one," and the app does not implement it. Building it needs the definition tightened and a validator pass first. This is Track B, not a same-day build.
